08 · Discipline

Do's and Don'ts

The rules that keep the system fresh instead of noisy.

Make every action keyboard-reachable, and surface the shortcut in its tooltip.
Don't rely on hue alone for status; pair it with an icon, shape, or text.
Treat ⌘K as primary navigation, not just a search box.
Don't paint large areas with pastel. Accents stay under 15% of the screen.
Build depth from layered surfaces; edges stay quiet and hairlines stay subtle.
Don't animate longer than 150ms. Hover feedback should feel immediate.
Keep controls compact (28–36px) so the writing canvas gets the room.
Don't introduce warm hues outside warning and error semantics.
Use mono for code, shortcuts, counts, and identifiers.
Don't go pure black. Crust is the floor; pure black clips the pastels.
